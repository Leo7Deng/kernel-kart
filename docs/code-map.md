# Kernel Kart: code map

The detailed map of every file and system (moved out of CLAUDE.md, which keeps a short version). Keep it current when you add or change a system.

## Code map
- `js/main.js`: entry and frame loop. `js/ui.js`: race start, loading, results, podium, Settings, cups and the `game` state (`clearForRace`, as any race goes on screen, offline, online or a time trial: a box left open over the last screen, which sits on the page and not in `#ui`, is closed by its own ✕ and the focus let go; online a race comes whenever the host starts it, and a What's new left open over it took an iPad's first tap); `showMenu(view)` hands the menu to `js/menu.js` (a flow of full screens: title → racers → map, or title → Online; below). Every menu choice lives in `setup`, saved as `kk-setup` (online also `kk-name` and the host's last room `kk-net-room`, net-ui.js). Players may share a racer (offline `setup.picks`, online `p.pick`; bots still take free ones, ui.js `botPool`), so nothing per racer may be keyed by `ch.id`: each racer has a seat `key` ('p0'.., 'b0'..; online 'p' + player id), copied onto its kart (`keyCars`, `hostStart`/`playerStart`; not in snapshots), and cups (pts, places, gain, `cupWire`) and the results/standings rows go by `rk(r)`. Karts on a shared racer get `twinLook` (art/cars.js): a player's in their `PCOL` paint with a ring of it on the road (render.js `twinRing`), a bot's darker; a racer of its own draws as before. `js/render.js`: drawing the race and HUD. Its camera (`viewsFor`) frames the human players, zooming out to keep everyone in shot, with one eased camera (state per race in `camState`, never on the race); never a split screen (the user tried one and asked for it gone for good, 2026-10-20). `render` draws the world through `drawWorld` (cached layers blitted to the view) and then the HUD. `js/view.js` (`VIEW`) is the world rect of the view being drawn, for art that culls or does parallax. `js/input.js`: keys and touch (`Input.held`: the keys down right now, so `reset` at a race's start keeps a gas key that's still held held, rather than ignoring it until it's pressed again: a player holding the gas as an online race came in couldn't drive, 2026-10-22; in a race only a text field, the chat line, keeps keys from the karts).
- `js/race.js`: `Race` and `Car`. `updateCar` is the per-kart physics (throttle, steering, movement, laps, ramps, run-off, timers). `collideCars` handles contact, including the Fast-Forward bowling, the sudo ram and the malloc squash. A kart's `L`, `W` and `weight` change while it's a malloc giant; `L0`, `W0`, `weight0` are its normal ones. `race-ai.js` is bot throttle (`aiThrottle`), `race-items.js` fires items, `race-hazards.js` runs trains (and `trainShy`, which keeps a bot's speed burst for after a crossing), `fx.js` spawns particles. `race-forks.js` is the line through a plain fork (Merge Bay): once your line and your kart are well over the halfway between the two roads, the steering eases over to steering by the sharp road, so a tucked kart turns in along it in one arc instead of jinking at the commit point; a shadow of the kart (`fkX`, `fkY`, ..., stepped by `forkShadow` with the steering as it was) is what `checkForks` decides by, so a given throttle gets the same road as before; `forkGuess` (through `splitGuess`) puts the dotted line and the minimap on the road you're set for. `race-start.js` is the rocket start (`startUpdate`, called from `update` until just after GO: each player's hold in `c.gasAt`, then `launch`, `rocket` or `wheelspin`; bots roll it on `car.rng` by skill). `race-bits.js` is the bits: their lines on the road (`bitSpots`, worked out from the track's shape and cached per track, not sent online), `bitsCar` (taking them), `spillBits` (called from `fxHit`, the one place every hit comes through, and from the moon capture), `bitLine` (a bot's lean toward one); state is `race.bits` (per spot: 0, or when it comes back; `[]` in a time trial, which has no bits), `race.loose` (thrown ones) and `c.bits`. All of these are mixed into `Race.prototype` at the bottom of `race.js`.
- Track mechanics that only some tracks have: `race-mechanics.js` builds them for a track (`T.mech`, null without them: then nothing below runs, so other tracks replay byte-identical), runs them per car (`mechCar`, one hook in `updateCar`), can give a corner its own steering line (`mechLine`: a slag zone with `wide` lets you carry speed on the outside and keeps you off slag that's down, the banking rides as high as your speed needs, the line holds upwind in the hurricane's crosswind, the line goes round hot lava, holds upwind in a jet's blast and leans onto a moving walkway; normally the line only goes from the middle to the inside), can change a car's grip where it is (`mechGrip`: the banking, the rain, the Moon), turns them into a bot speed plan (`mechAllowed`, one hook in `aiThrottle`), and runs whole-race state once a frame (`mechUpdate`: the hurricanes coming, the volcano's eruptions, the waves, the boulder timetable, the pour's roar, the crab, the gate shutting, the drawbridge's timetable and bell, the clocktower's strike, the rain's timetable and the dry line, the jets' timetable and their engines' roar). One file per mechanic: `race-wormhole.js`, `race-lowg.js` (with the moon and planets), `race-presses.js`, `race-slag.js`, `race-volcano.js`, `race-waves.js`, `race-hurricane.js`, `race-banks.js`, `race-jackpot.js`, `race-boulders.js`, `race-tunnel.js`, `race-crab.js`, `race-gate.js`, `race-drawbridge.js`, `race-secret.js` (secret shortcuts), `race-clock.js` (the clocktower's minute hand, pendulum and strike: all a pure function of the race clock, no state; its art is `art/clock.js`), `race-rain.js` (rain and the dry line), `race-jets.js` (jets taxiing and taking off: their bodies, their blast and the crossings), `race-belts.js` (moving walkways), `race-thicket.js` (Kernel Pretzel's Tinder Grove: a thicket the volcano burns open across a shortcut), `race-kickers.js` (Kernel Pretzel's Kicker Park: kickers across part of the road, and the lane round them), `race-rocket.js` (Moonshot's two rows, a rocket and a capsule for every racer, the rides, the Moon's grip: bays, docking, `flyPose`, `rocketPlan`), `race-timed.js` (timed obstacles, all a pure function of the clock: gantry `arms`, a test firing's `blast`, vehicles that `cross` the road (the crawler, the rover), airlock `doors`; `timedCar`, `timedLine` (down the middle through doors), `timedAI`: a bot simulates its run past them, `runPast`, and takes the soonest clean; Moonshot's launch complex and base), `race-crests.js` (Moonshot's Apennine Crests: local heights along a stretch (cosine bumps), going light over a crest (`crestGrip`, through `mechGrip`), floating off one past its float speed (`flight`: pressed to the road until it falls away clear of you), landings, each crest's turn and window `vFloat`/`vOk`, `crestsAI`), `race-canyon.js` (Moonshot's Hadley Rille: stretches walled in on both sides; `canyonRunoff`, through `mechRunoff`, race.js's one run-off hook (it also takes a secret's pocket), sets the run-off's limit to the rock; `canyonCar` the knock and the grind against it), `race-driver.js` (Moonshot's mass driver: a launch rail of coil rings on a beat, a pure function of the clock (`driverAt`: charging, firing, discharging); `driverCar` launches a kart through the first ring on the green (`c.mdV`, held down the rail) or grabs it on the red; `arrival` (a run in, flat out or holding a speed: the player's gauge and the bots); `driverAI`, last in `mechAllowed`'s chain, picks the speed that arrives when it pays most); Moonshot's crater bowl is a `race-banks.js` bank with `bowl` and a ground `grip`, each with its build, its rule and its bot logic. Per-race state lives on the race, never on `T.mech` (which is shared by every race on the track): `race.storms` (the hurricanes, a few numbers each), `race.volc`, `race.wav`, `race.rocks` (boulder release times), `race.crab`, `race.lifts` (when each drawbridge's bell rings), all drawn from `race.rng`, and `race.gate` (the logic gate's timer); `race.thicket` (when the volcano last threw a fireball at the thicket and when it landed, from race.rng; race-thicket.js), `race.planes` (the jets' timetable, a few numbers per departure from race.rng, race-jets.js); `race.bayN`, `race.outT`, `race.outN` and `race.cbayN`, `race.coutT`, `race.coutN` (Moonshot's Launch Row and Return Row: the next free bay, and when and in what turn the last kart booked rolls out, race-rocket.js); `race.rainT` (the rain's timetable, from `race.rng`) and `race.dryQ` (the dry line, a string republished from an accumulator kept off the race: race-rain.js). All their drawing is in `draw-mechanics.js` (static layer from `drawTrack`, animated layers from `render`, and `roofLayers`: things over the road that hide what's under them, the serpent's head and the temple, cached by `render` and laid over the karts; the jackpot's gold boxes and slot machine are drawn by `art/pharaoh.js`; the hurricane by `art/hurricane.js`; Sakura Stack's koi canal, drawbridge, torii (a roof layer) and drifting petals by `art/sakura.js`, which also has the `sakura` theme's props and its pagoda). Recursion Ridge adds `race-elevation.js` (the road's heights: gravity in `mechCar`, plus the only two hooks outside it, `elevTop` on the top speed in `updateCar` and the slope term in the bots' braking in `aiThrottle` and `mechAllowed`) and `race-cliff.js`; its terrain, the Cliff, its landmarks, the cable car, wind streaks and the Cliff's gauge are drawn by `art/alpine.js`. `race-ski.js` is Gradient Descent's ski run (see docs/tracks.md): its zones along the run (`T.mech.ski.zones`, sorted by s, at most one at any s: `glade`/`forest` (`field: true`, along a bend: `ins` its inside's side, `sF` where the sweep starts, `ways` the hidden ways through as lats every `FIELD.WS` (`wayLat`), the trees scattered off them, sorted by s), `slalom` (one spot per row, its pole `trees[]` with `pole`), `moguls` (`bumps` for the art, `hops` the hop rows), and the splits `park` and `ledge` (`split: true`; `lane(s)`: the lane's `edge`, `mid`, `in`, `far` (the island's far side), and for the Ledge its slope `m` and bend `k`, from `wig`, the shelf's shape: a chord across the bend plus two spurs); `M.park` / `M.ledge`, and `M.ramps` (the park's kickers and halfpipe walls and the moguls' hop rows, pushed into art/track.js `rampsOf` for drawing and the karts' pose; `own: true` ones are drawn by art/ski.js or not at all; a `peak` sets the hop's height in render.js `jumpPose`). In a field a kart's line across it is its own (`c.skiFZ` the field, `c.skiFL` / `c.skiFM` its lat and slope, from its speed, gas and drift: `fieldLine`, and its heading set to it: `fieldHeading`; `c.skiF` while in one). A kart's way through a zone with rows (the slalom, the park) is its own `c.skiW` = { wi, a (from), b (the spot), air } (picked by `skiPick` as it passes each row; after a park flight it starts again where it lands), its lane at a split `c.skiLane` ('piste', 'park', 'ledge'; a bot's choice `c.ai.skiLane` = the zone's t0), its line set straight onto `c.lineS` (`skiLine` through `steer`, last in `mechLine`; it sets `c.lineSet` (race.js then skips a bot's wander and a drift's straight-line pull) and `c.lineWide` (race.js lets the line term ask for more)), the drift's view of it (`skiK`, used by race-drift.js `nextSide`/`cornerness` through `kAt`; the strain counts from `SKI.TERM` of top speed there), the carve and the slide, the moguls' and the shelf's grip (`skiGrip`), the air's drag, the trees, the poles and missed gates, the mogul hops, the islands, the Ledge's lean, wall and edge (a fall), the park's launches, box and landings (`skiCar`), bots (`skiAI`, and `skiBotDrift`, which race-drift.js `driftAI` hands over to in a field (`fieldBotDrift`: following a way by drift on and off) or on a way with rows: slalom, park). `liftK` in a definition (race.js, race-ai.js: lifting slows you at that share of the usual rate) is its own hook.
  - `race-river.js` is Duke's Volcano's lava river, built after the volcano (`M.rivers = buildRivers(T, M.volcano)`: the channel's path from the crater's lip, its fords where it crosses the road, its lake). It has no timetable of its own: `riverUpdate` (in `mechUpdate`, after the volcano) watches `race.volc` for eruptions and, on race.rng, sends a surge (`race.surges`: per river, the eruption times whose lava is still coming down; `race.surgeSeen`). `riverCar` (in `mechCar`), `riverAI` (last in `mechAllowed`, after the crab) and `c.riverNear` (no boosts toward one: `straightAhead`, `wantUse`). Drawn by `riverStatic`/`riverFlow` in `draw-mechanics.js`, and the mountain under it by `volcanoFlank` (from `ground` in `art/track.js`, only on a track with `flank`).
  - Shared engine pieces first built for Epoch Expressway (the user had it removed 2026-10-21) stay: a very big map's cached layer in tiles (`tiles` in the definition: render.js `tiled`, a budget in millions of pixels cut into tiles under 4000 px a side, drawn in one go through a Proxy that passes every call to every tile's context: baked art for it must not set absolute transforms from `getTransform()`), a track that fixes its laps (`laps: 1`: Race's constructor, ui.js `startRace` and the cards, net.js `hostStart`, timetrial.js `ttPlan`; a one-lap race has no last-lap fanfare at the start), a railway that ends in tunnels (`train.ext: 0`: geometry.js only finds crossings and clears props along the line as far as `ext` past its ends), the bridge search through a grid (geometry.js: a very long lap took 14 s to build before), and `c.hazNear` (a hazard just ahead: keeps a bot's boosts off, `straightAhead`, `wantUse`).
- `js/items/<id>.js`: one module per item, holding text, `odds` per position tier, `use`, `wantUse` (bots), and any world objects it leaves (`updateProjectile`/`drawProjectile`, `updateWorld`/`drawWorld`). `items/index.js` is the registry; roll tables are built from `odds`. `items/mine.js` holds the mines shared by Segfault and Fork Bomb. `items/thread.js` (Thread) keeps its clones on `race.clones` (plain data, `clone: true`, `owner`): `updateWorld` runs from `Race.update` after `collideCars` (station just ahead of the owner on its side, `THREAD_AHEAD` 0.9 of its length, or straight in front where the road's too narrow; it spawns there, solid against karts by weight, boxes go to the owner through `race.giveItem`), the steering eases round them like a car, and hazards pop them with `hitClone` (duck, mines, slicks); Bubble and Pull Request may take a clone as a decoy (`clonesOf`). `items/loop.js` (Infinite Loop) keeps its loops on `race.loops`; a kart that drives into one gets `c.loopT` and `updateCar` hands it to `ride` (an early return, like the moon and the lava) until it's round; `steer` edges the line round a loop ahead, and `loopPose` is the cosmetic up-and-over pose render.js draws (timed on each screen, so it's smooth online).
- `js/race-drift.js`: the drift button, a third key per player (`KEYSETS` in core.js, `Input.drift`, touch `driftButtonRect`). On by default; the Drift setting (`settings.drift`, kk-settings) turns it off for the classic two-button game. Each race gets it as `cfg.drift` (`race.drift`, false unless given: the tests' races are drift-off): ui.js and timetrial-ui.js pass the setting, online the room's `cfg.drift` (the host's, a host-only room setting, sent in `start`). Off: `driftStep` never runs, `Input.driftOn` (set by render.js) ignores the key and touch button, and render.js hides the drift keycaps and button, so it's the pre-drift game exactly (`test/drift.mjs` checks holding the key changes nothing). Time trial records and ghosts with drift on are kept under `ttKey` (`<track> · drift`), apart from the old ones. A mini-turbo counts as a boost for a secret (`blazing`, race-secret.js). `driftStep` (from `updateCar`, every kart in a drift-on race; held is `Input.drift` for a player, `c.ai.drift` for a bot) runs the state (`c.dOn`, `c.dSide` picked at the press: the next corner's inside (a bot's: the corner it pressed for), `c.dAmt`, `c.dYaw` the drawn slide, `c.dCharge` and its tiers, `c.dStrain`, the mini-turbo `c.mtT`/`c.mtK`); `updateCar` reads it in five gated hooks (top speed, accel, grip, reach `tuck`, the straight's line pull). Nothing runs for a kart that never pressed it, and with drift off no bot ever presses it (`driftAI` isn't called, `c.rng` isn't drawn), so the golden is unchanged. Bots drift (2026-10-21, the user: "teach bots how to drift"): `driftAI` (race-drift.js, called at the end of `aiThrottle` on each reaction tick, bots only, drift on only) decides each corner once (`c.ai.dNext`): slow enough (`BOT.SLOW`), enough charge for a tier at the drift's speed (`BOT.DUR`, worked out by `nextCorner`), not an S, and clear of `noDriftZones` (every `T.mech.avoid` spot and any mechanic zone with s0..s1 from 650 before to 250 after, forks and splits, ramps, level crossings: the hazard plans, the Cliff, the gate tuck, banks, jackpots, the rocket rows are left alone); then with a chance by skill (`BOT.P0` 0.3 .. `P1` 0.9, rising as good^1.5: about half at L1) it sets `c.ai.dz` (the corner and the grip to plan on), its speed plan takes that corner at the drift's grip (`driftGrip`, in `aiThrottle`'s corner pass and `mechAllowed`'s re-plan; and the slide check is off while drifting), it presses `BOT.PRESS` 0.22 s before the corner and lets go at its end once the rest can be made on its own grip (a good bot hangs on for a tier a moment away). Worse bots plan on less of the grip (`PLAN0`), let go late (`LATE`), sometimes early without the grip for the rest (`SLOPPY`), and now and then hold it into a spin-out (`OVER`), all on `c.rng`. It lets go for anything else (a hazard flag, `stayWide`/`tuckIn`/`carry`/`secretGo`, a boost, off, in the air). `node test/drift-bots.mjs` measures it (solo and four-bot races, drift on vs off), `node test/drift-corners.mjs` what each drift is worth by kind of corner, `npm run balance -- 640 --drift` the balance with it. Online the input's `g` carries drift in bit 2 and the prediction drives it. Drawn by render.js (body turned about `DRIFT_PIVOT` via `driftPt`, counter-steer, rider lean, skid marks) and fx.js (smoke, sparks, `driftGlow`, the mini-turbo flame). What it is worth: `node test/drift.mjs [tracks]`.
- The Style setting (`settings.look`, kk-settings): 'classic' (the default, the glossy look, exactly style.css), or one of three flat looks that share every code path and differ in palette, fonts and a few shapes: 'flat' (**Classic Flat**: Classic's layout, indigo, red-orange and gold, Bungee + Fredoka, round corners, skewed badges and inked, extruded titles, with the gradients, glows, gloss, shine sweeps, spotlights, sunburst and rainbows taken out: solid fills, simple soft or hard-offset shadows, a gentle two-tone at most on a big surface), 'racing' (**Racing**: black/carbon panels, racing red #e8141c, signal yellow #ffd400 and white, Barlow Condensed in heavy italic capitals for titles and numbers over Barlow, square corners, skewed red number plates on the section headers, a red/yellow stripe on each panel's top edge, a chequered strip fading along the header line, a skewed START with yellow chevrons, racing stripes on the racers' backdrops, a chequered Random card, number plates for places in the results, HUD and standings, a chequer strip on the start gantry and the winner banner) and 'premium' (**Premium**: espresso #1d1612 panels with a very subtle warm shade down big surfaces and a faint warm top highlight, amber #ec9a3e / copper accent with dark text on it, warm off-white text, Montserrat spaced capitals over Manrope, 12-16px corners, soft shadows). A stored 'bold' (the earlier Bold, Cartoon and Clean styles, all rejected: the stark one hurt the eyes, the soft one "not it", Clean "still too plain ... I don't like the super plain light blue") loads as 'flat' (settings.js). The user (2026-10-20) wants an alternative to Classic because Classic looks "AI-generated" (gradients, rainbow and glows everywhere): keep the flat looks free of glows, rainbows, gloss and text outlines (Classic Flat keeps Classic's own ink outlines), effects sparing, no words in effects. `js/look.js` (DOM-free): `LOOKS` (value, name), `bold()` (any flat look: the old name, kept for the call sites), `lookIs(name)` (a look's own touch), the palette `BK` (swapped in place by `setLook`; keys: `panel`/`panelRGB` the HUD's see-through panel, `surface`/`surface2` solid cards and chips, `well` the item slot, `line`, `hi` the top highlight, `text`/`muted`/`dim`, `ink` the dark edge (icons' thin outlines, lamp housings), `shade`, `accent` and the status colours, `lampOff`, `mini` the minimap road, `box`/`boxSide`/`boxMark` the item box, `plate`/`plateText` number plates, `onAccent`, `r` the largest HUD corner radius), the fonts as live bindings `BOLD_FONT` (text), `BOLD_DISPLAY` (titles and numbers) and `BOLD_W` (its style and weight prefix, e.g. 'italic 800 '; '' for Bungee), `lookFonts()` (the look's Google Fonts URL and the faces a canvas needs), `bbox`, `bpanel` (a HUD panel: soft two-fill shadow, the box, corners capped at `BK.r`, Premium's warm sheen), `chequer` and `plate` (Racing), `boldCol`, `lookGen()` (changes on every switch). Read `BK` and the fonts when drawing, never at module load (draw-timetrial.js builds its palette per `lookGen`). DOM: ui.js `applyLook` sets `<html data-look>` (and `data-modern` for Racing and Premium) and fetches only the picked look's fonts (Classic Flat uses the Bungee/Fredoka index.html already loads), then calls `setLook` again once they're in so font-drawn sprites redraw. CSS: style.css is Classic and untouched by the others; `look-flat.css` is Classic Flat as overrides on it (`[data-look=flat]`); `look-modern.css` is the shared base for Racing and Premium (`[data-modern]`, structure only: every colour, font and radius is a variable, `--ground`, `--panel-bg`, `--card`, `--accent`, `--on-accent`, `--gold`, `--on-gold`, `--display`, `--body`, `--r-panel`, `--r-card`, `--r-btn`, `--big-bg`...), then `look-racing.css` and `look-premium.css` (their variables and own shapes). The logo SVG (art/logo.js) has classes (`kk-ex`, `kk-ol`, `kk-w`, `kk-g`, `kk-gloss`, `kk-swoosh`, `kk-speed`, `kk-rib`, `kk-sq`...) so each look restyles it. Canvas: drawing code branches on `bold()` (render.js HUD `BD`, with `RC` for Racing's plates and stripes; draw-timetrial.js `pal()` and `tabText`; fx.js `drawParticlesBold` and the status effects; the items' glows; keycaps in art.js): flat fills from `BK`, no outlines on effects, no `lighter`, no gradients or glow sprites, no new effects. Item icons in a flat look go through `drawIconBold` (icons.js: drawn once in paint.js's flat mode, icons.js's `setFlat` wrapper, where `linear`/`radial` return a flat colour, `shine` is skipped, `lw` is thinner and `INK` is `BK.ink`, cached per size and `lookGen()`); `itemBoxesBold` is the box (a flat tile in `BK.box`, the ? in `BK.boxMark`). Every sprite made in a look keys on `lookGen()`. New drawing code: keep classic as it was and give the flat looks one flat branch reading `BK` and the fonts; add a `lookIs()` touch only where a look really needs its own shape. It's drawing only: nothing in a race may read it.
- `js/clip.js` + `js/clip-ui.js`: the menu's "Watch how" clips (Cron Canyon's mine and Pyth Plateau's phoenix, both on a drift). `clip.js` (DOM-free) is the clip: a real `Race` on a fixed seed (its own `Math.random` while it builds and steps, and `sfx.play` silenced), one human kart alone, no boxes or bits, driven by scripted keys (the bots' throttle up to `press`, then gas flat and drift held until `letGo` s after blue), run unseen to `from` and shown to `tail` s after it's back on the road; positions are s on the clip's `lap` (default 1; `clipAt`, negative before its line), so a lap-3 secret (the phoenix) runs laps 1-2 unseen too (~3000 steps, 30-130 ms); captions per step are the clip's `say`; `race.clip` makes render.js leave the standings off. `sim.test.mjs` runs every clip and fails if it no longer takes the kart into the secret. `clip-ui.js` is the modal over the menu (every look, style.css and the look-*.css files `.clip-*`): steps it at a fixed 1/60 s, draws it with render.js `renderTo` (render's canvas, size and track-layer cache swapped for the clip's canvas for the frame and put back, so the game's are untouched), the gas and drift keycaps (`drawKeycap`) and a caption; ½ speed, Replay (R), loops, Esc or ✕ closes and lets it all go. Offered by the map's How it plays (menu.js `drawPreview`, a Watch how button), offline only (never in an online room).
- `js/frontrun.js`: the Front-runner meter under each track's Show more (ui.js `trackText` calls `frontrunHTML(t)`, and the map's How it plays, menu.js `drawPreview`; menu.js `frBadge` draws the compact one on every tile while `setup.showFR` is on, sorted by `setup.frSort` 0 usual / 1 highest / 2 lowest; style.css `.fr*`, in the text's own colour with gold pips, so it reads in every look). Its numbers are `js/frontrun-data.js`, generated by `test/frontrun.mjs` (`npm run frontrun`; how it scores: docs/balance.md).
- `js/rules.js`: `untouchable(c)` (ghost, fast-forward, a malloc giant, up an Infinite Loop: no hazard touches them), `phased(c)` (the same without the giant: what items pass through; a giant tanks them), `grassSlows(c)`, `blockedByShield(race, c)` (a Firewall soaks up one hit; a malloc giant every one). Use these instead of writing the checks again.
- `js/art/`: all the drawing, lit from the top left (shadows fall down-right). `art.js` re-exports it (plus `drawItemIcon`, `drawKeycap`, `FONT`).
  - `cars.js`: `drawCar`, one real vehicle per body in realistic paint (`CAR_PAINT`), drawn at a design size and stretched to the `BODIES` footprint, so the car you see is the hitbox. The shield (`block`: guard held, no direction at the press): covers `f.guardArc` either side of the facing (70°, Ferris 100°); a hit from inside it chips `chip` (10 %) of its damage, costs `blockStam` x damage of stamina (x `blockCost`), pushes both back, holds the blocker in block stun (`f.stun`, still state 'block') and puts a contact attacker straight into its recovery (event 'block'; `stagger` true when it broke a gassed guard); from outside the arc it lands as a clean hit. No perfect block, no pool (`guardHp` stays 1 for old readers). A clean hit: a counter hit (the victim in `wind` or `charge`) x `counter` 1.4, a flank (`side` past `sideArc` 70° off their facing x 1.2, `back` past `backArc` 120° x 1.4), together at most `bonusCap` 1.8 (events 'counter' and 'flank' with `side`: who the attacker, to the victim; `f.counter`, `f.flankT`/`f.flankSide` on the victim for the drawer; 'hit' carries `counter`, `side`, `dmg`); hitstun (`hurt`) is the move's `stun` or 0.22 + kb/1200, x `comboScale` (x(1 - `comboDecay` per hit in the combo), at least `comboMin`), longer on a counter or from behind; hitstop 3-8 frames. Knockdowns (`knockDown`): a full charge, a move with `kd`, a chain's finisher on a counter or a flank, knockback over `downKb`, a wall slam, a heat vent: `down` for `downT` (sliding; `f.down` true), then `getup` for `getupT` (untouchable); nothing hits a fighter down or getting up (events 'down' with `by` (-1 the map), 'getup', who the one going down). Steps (`stepStart`, guard pressed with a direction): `stepT` s, `stepDist` units, kept relative to the foe (`f.stepRel`, so a sideways one circles them; `f.stepA` its way now), `stepCd` s apart, no invincibility (event 'step' with `a`); a light or heavy during a forward one is a step attack (`f.stepAtk` true: +`stepReach`, +`stepStartup`). Combos: lights chain (a buffered light in a chainable light's recovery), a light that landed clean (`f.conf`) cancels into the heavy, special or super (`confirm(f)`, `f.cancel`: startup x `cancelStartup`), stale moves (`f.recent`, `stale` less per repeat). Trades: a direct hit on someone whose own contact move is active and reaches back lands both ways, each at `trade` x knockback (event 'trade'). Armor (`armorOf(f, prio)`): light armor (Tux's flop, a heavy held half-charged, Docky's Container) soaks lights in startup, super armor (supers) soaks everything (event 'armor'); the damage counts, no flinch, no counter bonus.
