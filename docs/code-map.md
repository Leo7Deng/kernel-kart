# Kernel Kart: code map

Every file and system in detail (moved out of CLAUDE.md, which keeps a short version). Keep it current when you add or change a system.

## Code map
- `js/main.js`: entry and frame loop. `js/ui.js`: race start, loading, results, podium, Settings, cups and the `game` state (`clearForRace`, as any race goes on screen, offline, online or time trial: a box left open over the last screen, on the page not in `#ui`, is closed by its own ✕ and focus let go; online a race comes whenever the host starts it, and a What's new left open over it took an iPad's first tap); `showMenu(view)` hands the menu to `js/menu.js` (full screens: title → racers → map, or title → Online; below). Every menu choice lives in `setup`, saved as `kk-setup` (online also `kk-name` and the host's last room `kk-net-room`, net-ui.js). Players may share a racer (offline `setup.picks`, online `p.pick`; bots still take free ones, ui.js `botPool`), so never key anything per racer by `ch.id`: each racer has a seat `key` ('p0'.., 'b0'..; online 'p' + player id), copied onto its kart (`keyCars`, `hostStart`/`playerStart`; not in snapshots); cups (pts, places, gain, `cupWire`) and results/standings rows go by `rk(r)`. Karts on a shared racer get `twinLook` (art/cars.js): a player's in their `PCOL` paint (no ring on the road any more), a bot's darker; an unshared racer draws as before. `js/render.js`: the race and HUD. Its camera (`viewsFor`) frames the human players, zooming out to keep everyone in shot, one eased camera (state per race in `camState`, never on the race); never split screen (the user tried one and wanted it gone for good, 2026-10-20). `render` draws the world via `drawWorld` (cached layers blitted to the view), then the HUD. `js/view.js` (`VIEW`): world rect of the view being drawn, for art that culls or does parallax. `js/input.js`: keys and touch (`Input.held`: keys down now, so `reset` at race start keeps a still-held gas key held instead of ignoring it until pressed again: a player holding gas as an online race came in couldn't drive, 2026-10-22; in a race only a text field, the chat line, keeps keys from the karts).
- `js/race.js`: `Race` and `Car`. `updateCar` is the per-kart physics (throttle, steering, movement, laps, ramps, run-off, timers). `collideCars` handles contact, including the Fast-Forward bowling, the sudo ram and the malloc squash. A kart's `L`, `W` and `weight` change while it's a malloc giant; `L0`, `W0`, `weight0` are its normal ones. `race-ai.js`: bot throttle (`aiThrottle`); `race-items.js` fires items; `race-hazards.js` runs trains (and `trainShy`, saving a bot's speed burst for after a crossing); `fx.js` spawns particles. `race-forks.js`: the line through a plain fork (Merge Bay): once line and kart are well past halfway between the two roads, steering eases over to the sharp road, so a tucked kart turns in along it in one arc instead of jinking at the commit point; `checkForks` decides by a shadow kart (`fkX`, `fkY`, ..., stepped by `forkShadow` with the steering as it was), so a given throttle gets the same road as before; `forkGuess` (via `splitGuess`) puts the dotted line and minimap on the road you're set for. `race-start.js`: the rocket start (`startUpdate`, called from `update` until just after GO: each player's hold in `c.gasAt`, then `launch`, `rocket` or `wheelspin`; bots roll it on `car.rng` by skill). `race-bits.js`: bits: their lines on the road (`bitSpots`, from the track's shape, cached per track, not sent online), `bitsCar` (taking them), `spillBits` (called from `fxHit`, which every hit comes through, and from the moon capture), `bitLine` (a bot's lean toward one); state is `race.bits` (per spot: 0, or when it comes back; `[]` in a time trial, which has no bits), `race.loose` (thrown ones) and `c.bits`. All of these are mixed into `Race.prototype` at the bottom of `race.js`.
- Track mechanics only some tracks have: `race-mechanics.js` builds them (`T.mech`, null without them: nothing below runs, so other tracks replay byte-identical), runs them per car (`mechCar`, one hook in `updateCar`), can give a corner its own steering line (`mechLine`: a slag zone with `wide` carries speed on the outside and keeps you off slag that's down; banking rides as high as your speed needs; holds upwind in a hurricane's crosswind or a jet's blast; goes round hot lava; leans onto a moving walkway; normally the line only goes middle to inside), can change grip where a car is (`mechGrip`: banking, rain, the Moon), makes a bot speed plan (`mechAllowed`, one hook in `aiThrottle`), and runs whole-race state each frame (`mechUpdate`: hurricanes coming, volcano eruptions, waves, boulder timetable, the pour's roar, the crab, the gate shutting, drawbridge timetable and bell, clocktower strike, rain timetable and dry line, jets' timetable and engine roar). One file per mechanic, each with build, rule and bot logic: `race-wormhole.js`, `race-lowg.js` (with the moon and planets), `race-presses.js`, `race-slag.js`, `race-volcano.js`, `race-waves.js`, `race-hurricane.js`, `race-banks.js`, `race-jackpot.js`, `race-boulders.js`, `race-tunnel.js`, `race-crab.js`, `race-gate.js`, `race-drawbridge.js`, `race-secret.js` (secret shortcuts), `race-clock.js` (clocktower's minute hand, pendulum, strike: pure function of the race clock, no state; art in `art/clock.js`), `race-rain.js` (rain, dry line), `race-jets.js` (jets taxiing and taking off: bodies, blast, crossings), `race-belts.js` (moving walkways), `race-thicket.js` (Kernel Pretzel's Tinder Grove: a thicket over a shortcut that the volcano burns open), `race-kickers.js` (Kernel Pretzel's Kicker Park: kickers across part of the road, and the lane round them), `race-rocket.js` (Moonshot's two rows, a rocket and a capsule per racer, the rides, Moon grip: bays, docking, `flyPose`, `rocketPlan`), `race-timed.js` (timed obstacles, pure function of the clock: gantry `arms`, a test firing's `blast`, vehicles that `cross` the road (crawler, rover), airlock `doors`; `timedCar`, `timedLine` (down the middle through doors), `timedAI`: a bot simulates its run past them (`runPast`) and takes the soonest clean; Moonshot's launch complex and base), `race-crests.js` (Moonshot's Apennine Crests: local heights along a stretch (cosine bumps), going light over a crest (`crestGrip`, via `mechGrip`), floating off past its float speed (`flight`: pressed to the road until it falls away clear of you), landings, each crest's turn and window `vFloat`/`vOk`, `crestsAI`), `race-canyon.js` (Moonshot's Hadley Rille: stretches walled both sides; `canyonRunoff`, via `mechRunoff` (race.js's one run-off hook, also takes a secret's pocket), limits run-off to the rock; `canyonCar`: knock and grind against it), `race-driver.js` (Moonshot's mass driver: a launch rail of coil rings on a beat, pure function of the clock (`driverAt`: charging, firing, discharging); `driverCar` launches a kart through the first ring on green (`c.mdV`, held down the rail) or grabs it on red; `arrival` (a run in, flat out or holding a speed: player's gauge and bots); `driverAI`, last in `mechAllowed`'s chain, picks the speed that arrives when it pays most); Moonshot's crater bowl is a `race-banks.js` bank with `bowl` and a ground `grip`. Per-race state lives on the race, never on `T.mech` (shared by every race on the track). From `race.rng`: `race.storms` (hurricanes, a few numbers each), `race.volc`, `race.wav`, `race.rocks` (boulder release times), `race.crab`, `race.lifts` (when each drawbridge's bell rings), `race.thicket` (when the volcano last threw a fireball at the thicket and when it landed; race-thicket.js), `race.planes` (jets' timetable, a few numbers per departure; race-jets.js), `race.rainT` (rain timetable). Others: `race.gate` (logic gate's timer); `race.bayN`, `race.outT`, `race.outN` and `race.cbayN`, `race.coutT`, `race.coutN` (Moonshot's Launch Row and Return Row: next free bay, and when and in what turn the last booked kart rolls out; race-rocket.js); `race.dryQ` (dry line, a string republished from an accumulator kept off the race; race-rain.js). Their drawing is all in `draw-mechanics.js` (static layer from `drawTrack`, animated layers from `render`, and `roofLayers`: things over the road hiding what's under them (serpent's head, temple), cached by `render`, laid over the karts); jackpot's gold boxes and slot machine in `art/pharaoh.js`; hurricane in `art/hurricane.js`; Sakura Stack's koi canal, drawbridge, torii (a roof layer) and drifting petals in `art/sakura.js` (also the `sakura` theme's props and pagoda). Recursion Ridge adds `race-elevation.js` (road heights: gravity in `mechCar`, plus the only two hooks outside it: `elevTop` on top speed in `updateCar`, the slope term in bot braking in `aiThrottle` and `mechAllowed`) and `race-cliff.js`; its terrain, Cliff, landmarks, cable car, wind streaks and Cliff gauge are drawn by `art/alpine.js`. `race-ski.js`: Gradient Descent's ski run (see docs/tracks.md). Zones (`T.mech.ski.zones`, sorted by s, at most one at any s): `glade`/`forest` (`field: true`, along a bend: `ins` its inside's side, `sF` where the sweep starts, `ways` the hidden ways through as lats every `FIELD.WS` (`wayLat`), trees scattered off them, sorted by s), `slalom` (one spot per row, pole `trees[]` with `pole`), `moguls` (`bumps` for art, `hops` the hop rows), and splits `park` and `ledge` (`split: true`; `lane(s)`: the lane's `edge`, `mid`, `in`, `far` (the island's far side), and for the Ledge its slope `m` and bend `k`, from `wig`, the shelf's shape: a chord across the bend plus two spurs); `M.park` / `M.ledge`, and `M.ramps` (park kickers, halfpipe walls and mogul hop rows, pushed into art/track.js `rampsOf` for drawing and kart pose; `own: true` ones drawn by art/ski.js or not at all; a `peak` sets hop height in render.js `jumpPose`). In a field each kart has its own line across it (`c.skiFZ` the field, `c.skiFL` / `c.skiFM` its lat and slope, from speed, gas and drift: `fieldLine`; heading set to it: `fieldHeading`; `c.skiF` while in one). In a zone with rows (slalom, park) its own way `c.skiW` = { wi, a (from), b (the spot), air } (picked by `skiPick` at each row; after a park flight restarted where it lands); its lane at a split `c.skiLane` ('piste', 'park', 'ledge'; a bot's choice `c.ai.skiLane` = the zone's t0); its line set straight onto `c.lineS` (`skiLine` via `steer`, last in `mechLine`; sets `c.lineSet` (race.js then skips a bot's wander and a drift's straight-line pull) and `c.lineWide` (race.js lets the line term ask for more)); the drift's view of it (`skiK`, used by race-drift.js `nextSide`/`cornerness` via `kAt`; strain counts from `SKI.TERM` of top speed there); carve and slide, moguls' and shelf's grip (`skiGrip`), air drag, trees, poles and missed gates, mogul hops, islands, the Ledge's lean, wall and edge (a fall), the park's launches, box and landings (`skiCar`); bots (`skiAI`, and `skiBotDrift`, which race-drift.js `driftAI` hands over to in a field (following a way by drift on and off) or on a way with rows: slalom, park). `liftK` in a definition (race.js, race-ai.js: lifting slows you at that share of the usual rate) is its own hook.
  - `race-river.js`: Duke's Volcano's lava river, built after the volcano (`M.rivers = buildRivers(T, M.volcano)`: channel path from the crater's lip, fords where it crosses the road, its lake). No timetable of its own: `riverUpdate` (in `mechUpdate`, after the volcano) watches `race.volc` for eruptions and, on race.rng, sends a surge (`race.surges`: per river, eruption times whose lava is still coming down; `race.surgeSeen`). `riverCar` (in `mechCar`), `riverAI` (last in `mechAllowed`, after the crab) and `c.riverNear` (no boosts toward one: `straightAhead`, `wantUse`). Drawn by `riverStatic`/`riverFlow` in `draw-mechanics.js`, the mountain under it by `volcanoFlank` (from `ground` in `art/track.js`, only on a track with `flank`).
  - Engine pieces first built for Epoch Expressway (removed by the user 2026-10-21) stay: a very big map's cached layer in tiles (`tiles` in the definition: render.js `tiled`, a budget in millions of pixels cut into tiles under 4000 px a side, drawn in one go via a Proxy passing every call to every tile's context, so baked art for it must not set absolute transforms from `getTransform()`), fixed laps (`laps: 1`: Race's constructor, ui.js `startRace` and the cards, net.js `hostStart`, timetrial.js `ttPlan`; a one-lap race has no last-lap fanfare at the start), a railway ending in tunnels (`train.ext: 0`: geometry.js only finds crossings and clears props along the line up to `ext` past its ends), the bridge search through a grid (geometry.js; a very long lap took 14 s to build before), and `c.hazNear` (a hazard just ahead: keeps a bot's boosts off, `straightAhead`, `wantUse`).
- `js/items/<id>.js`: one module per item: text, `odds` per position tier, `use`, `wantUse` (bots), and any world objects it leaves (`updateProjectile`/`drawProjectile`, `updateWorld`/`drawWorld`). `items/index.js` is the registry; roll tables are built from `odds`. `items/mine.js` holds the mines shared by Segfault and Fork Bomb. `items/thread.js` (Thread) keeps its clones on `race.clones` (plain data, `clone: true`, `owner`): `updateWorld` runs from `Race.update` after `collideCars` (station just ahead of the owner on its side, `THREAD_AHEAD` 0.9 of its length, or straight in front where the road's too narrow; spawns there, solid against karts by weight, boxes go to the owner via `race.giveItem`); steering eases round them like a car; hazards pop them with `hitClone` (duck, mines, slicks); Bubble and Pull Request may take a clone as a decoy (`clonesOf`). `items/loop.js` (Infinite Loop) keeps its loops on `race.loops`; a kart driving into one gets `c.loopT` and `updateCar` hands it to `ride` (an early return, like the moon and lava) until it's round; `steer` edges the line round a loop ahead; `loopPose` is the cosmetic up-and-over pose render.js draws (timed per screen, so smooth online).
- `js/race-drift.js`: the drift button, a third key per player (`KEYSETS` in core.js, `Input.drift`, touch `driftButtonRect`). On by default; the Drift setting (`settings.drift`, kk-settings) turns it off for the classic two-button game. Each race gets it as `cfg.drift` (`race.drift`, false unless given: test races are drift-off): ui.js and timetrial-ui.js pass the setting, online the room's `cfg.drift` (the host's, a host-only room setting, sent in `start`). Off: `driftStep` never runs, `Input.driftOn` (set by render.js) ignores key and touch button, render.js hides the drift keycaps and button: exactly the pre-drift game (`test/drift.mjs` checks holding the key changes nothing). Time trial records and ghosts with drift on are kept apart under `ttKey` (`<track> · drift`). A mini-turbo counts as a boost for a secret (`blazing`, race-secret.js). `driftStep` (from `updateCar`, every kart in a drift-on race; held is `Input.drift` for a player, `c.ai.drift` for a bot) runs the state (`c.dOn`, `c.dSide` picked at the press: the next corner's inside (a bot's: the corner it pressed for), `c.dAmt`, `c.dYaw` the drawn slide, `c.dCharge` and its tiers, `c.dStrain`, mini-turbo `c.mtT`/`c.mtK`); `updateCar` reads it in five gated hooks (top speed, accel, grip, reach `tuck`, the straight's line pull). Nothing runs for a kart that never pressed it, and with drift off no bot presses it (`driftAI` isn't called, `c.rng` isn't drawn), so the golden is unchanged. Bots drift (2026-10-21, the user: "teach bots how to drift"): `driftAI` (end of `aiThrottle` each reaction tick, bots only, drift on only) decides each corner once (`c.ai.dNext`): slow enough (`BOT.SLOW`), enough charge for a tier at the drift's speed (`BOT.DUR`, from `nextCorner`), not an S, clear of `noDriftZones` (every `T.mech.avoid` spot and any mechanic zone with s0..s1 from 650 before to 250 after, forks and splits, ramps, level crossings: hazard plans, the Cliff, the gate tuck, banks, jackpots, rocket rows are left alone); then by a skill chance (`BOT.P0` 0.3 .. `P1` 0.9, rising as good^1.5: about half at L1) sets `c.ai.dz` (the corner and grip to plan on); its speed plan takes that corner at the drift's grip (`driftGrip`, in `aiThrottle`'s corner pass and `mechAllowed`'s re-plan; slide check off while drifting); presses `BOT.PRESS` 0.22 s before the corner and lets go at its end once the rest can be made on its own grip (a good bot hangs on for a tier a moment away). Worse bots plan on less grip (`PLAN0`), let go late (`LATE`), sometimes early without the grip for the rest (`SLOPPY`), now and then hold it into a spin-out (`OVER`), all on `c.rng`. Lets go for anything else (a hazard flag, `stayWide`/`tuckIn`/`carry`/`secretGo`, a boost, off, in the air). Measured by `node test/drift-bots.mjs` (solo and four-bot races, drift on vs off), `node test/drift-corners.mjs` (each drift's worth by kind of corner), `npm run balance -- 640 --drift` (balance with it). Online the input's `g` carries drift in bit 2 and the prediction drives it. Drawn by render.js (body turned about `DRIFT_PIVOT` via `driftPt`, counter-steer, rider lean, skid marks) and fx.js (smoke, sparks, `driftGlow`, mini-turbo flame). What it's worth: `node test/drift.mjs [tracks]`.
- `kk-ui.css`: the shared screen look (Fight's screens; the main menu can follow it). - `js/clip.js` + `js/clip-ui.js`: the menu's "Watch how" clips (Cron Canyon's mine and Pyth Plateau's phoenix, both on a drift). `clip.js` (DOM-free): a real `Race` on a fixed seed (own `Math.random` while it builds and steps, `sfx.play` silenced), one human kart alone, no boxes or bits, driven by scripted keys (bots' throttle up to `press`, then gas flat and drift held until `letGo` s after blue), run unseen to `from`, shown until `tail` s after it's back on the road; positions are s on the clip's `lap` (default 1; `clipAt`, negative before its line), so a lap-3 secret (the phoenix) runs laps 1-2 unseen too (~3000 steps, 30-130 ms); per-step captions in the clip's `say`; `race.clip` makes render.js leave the standings off. `sim.test.mjs` runs every clip and fails if one no longer takes the kart into the secret. `clip-ui.js`: the modal over the menu (every look; style.css and the look-*.css files `.clip-*`): steps it at a fixed 1/60 s, draws it with render.js `renderTo` (render's canvas, size and track-layer cache swapped for the clip's for the frame and put back, so the game's are untouched), plus gas and drift keycaps (`drawKeycap`) and a caption; ½ speed, Replay (R), loops; Esc or ✕ closes and lets it all go. Offered by the map's How it plays (menu.js `drawPreview`, a Watch how button), offline only (never in an online room).
- `js/frontrun.js`: the Front-runner meter under each track's Show more (ui.js `trackText` calls `frontrunHTML(t)`, and the map's How it plays, menu.js `drawPreview`; menu.js `frBadge` draws the compact one on every tile while `setup.showFR` is on, sorted by `setup.frSort` 0 usual / 1 highest / 2 lowest; style.css `.fr*`, in the text's own colour with gold pips, so it reads in every look). Numbers in `js/frontrun-data.js`, generated by `test/frontrun.mjs` (`npm run frontrun`; how it scores: docs/balance.md).
- `js/rules.js`: `untouchable(c)` (ghost, fast-forward, a malloc giant, up an Infinite Loop: no hazard touches them), `phased(c)` (same without the giant: what items pass through; a giant tanks them), `grassSlows(c)`, `blockedByShield(race, c)` (a Firewall soaks up one hit; a malloc giant every one). Use these instead of writing the checks again.
- `js/art/`: all the drawing, lit from the top left (shadows fall down-right). `art.js` re-exports it (plus `drawItemIcon`, `drawKeycap`, `FONT`).
  - `cars.js`: `drawCar`, one real vehicle per body in realistic paint (`CAR_PAINT`), drawn at a design size and stretched to the `BODIES` footprint, so the car you see is the hitbox. ## Type checks (2026-10-30)
The game stays plain JavaScript (ES modules, Vite, no type build step); TypeScript's checker reads its JSDoc types, so a helper's merge that breaks a shape another module relies on (a kit hook's arguments, a fighter or car field, a snapshot's fields) shows before a test runs.
- **Run:** `npm run typecheck` (`tsc -p jsconfig.json --noEmit`, TypeScript 7, the native one: under a second for all of `js/`), the last step of `npm test`. Must stay at 0 errors. After `git pull`, `npm install` once (it's a devDependency).
- **What's checked:** `jsconfig.json` takes in every file under `js/` (types flow from anything imported) but reports only on files starting with `// @ts-check`: `fight.js`, `fight-ai.js`, `fight-style.js`, `fight-net.js`, `fight-practice.js`, `fight-arenas.js`, every `kits/*.js`, `race.js`, `geometry.js`, `race-mechanics.js`, `detmath.js`, `net.js`, `net-sync.js`, `net-room.js`. Editors reading `jsconfig.json` (VS Code, Zed) show the same errors as you type. `strict` off (no `noImplicitAny` or `strictNullChecks`: hundreds of noise errors each), the cheap ones on (`strictBindCallApply`, `strictFunctionTypes`, `noImplicitThis`, `noFallthroughCasesInSwitch`...).
- **Where the shared types live** (each `@typedef` next to what owns the shape; import one with `import('./kits/index.js').Kit`):
  - the kit API, `js/kits/index.js`: `Plugin` (a kit file's default export), `FighterData` (its `fighter`, FIGHTERS[id]), `Move`, `Moves`, `Clip`, `Kit` (a move's hooks), `ObjStep`, `BotHooks` (its `ai`), `BotCtx` (the bot hooks' `C`), `Seen` (the foe as a bot sees it: only perceive's fields, so reading one it doesn't carry is an error), `Threat`, `PunishWindow`, `NeutralOffer`, `Walk`, `Contact`;
  - the fight, `js/fight.js`: `FightConfig`, `FightInput`, `Ctl`, `Fighter` (every core field typed, a kit's own `fields` allowed as extras), `WorldObj`, `FightEvent`, `HitOpt`; the arenas' `Arena`, `Shape`, `Solid`, `Hazard` in `fight-arenas.js`; the wire's `PackedInput`, `FightSnap`, `FightNetOpts` in `fight-net.js`;
  - the race: `Track`, `TrackDef`, `Mech`, `TrackPoint` in `geometry.js`; `Racer`, `RaceConfig` and the exported `Car` class in `race.js`; online `Snap`, `NetInput`, `HostSyncOpts`, `ClientSyncOpts` in `net-sync.js`, `Session`, `RoomPlayer` in `net.js`, `RoomCfg`, `Room`, `RoomAsk`, `RoomAnswer` in `net-room.js`.
  - `js/types.d.ts`, checker only (never loaded by the game): what JSDoc can't say. Race's methods mixed in from other files (the `Object.assign(Race.prototype, ...)` at the bottom of race.js: a new mixin object goes in both), race fields a mixin makes that a checked file reads (`goT`, `packMode`, `net`, `localHumans`, `bits`, `loose`, `gate`, `crab`), `Car.T`, and Fight's non-enumerable `A` and `rngs`.
- **Conventions:** a function or method other modules call gets `/** @param {Type} name ... @returns {Type} */` (several on one line is fine); an exported arrow `/** @type {(a: A) => B} */`; an object of methods mixed into Race `/** @satisfies {ThisType<import('./race.js').Race>} */` (race-mechanics.js `mechanics`). Shapes are TS object types in the `@typedef` (`{{ x: number, [k: string]: any }}`: the index signature where kits or mechanics hang their own fields). A kit types its four parts: `/** @type {import('./index.js').FighterData} */ const fighter`, `Record<string, import('./index.js').Kit>` for `kits`, `Record<string, import('./index.js').ObjStep>` for `objs`, `import('./index.js').BotHooks` for `ai` (`_template.js` has them). Types are comments, never a reason to change runtime behaviour. A real bug the checker finds is fixed in its own commit (fights replay identically, golden unchanged); one whose fix would change play gets `// @ts-expect-error: why` and is reported.
- **New code:** a new module in the cores above (fight module, kit, race or online core file) starts with `// @ts-check` and types its exports; a new kit or bot hook goes into `Kit` / `BotHooks` with the header's description. Other files may opt in likewise.

- Combat in short (numbers in fight.js `RULES`; docs/fight-frames.md has every move phase by phase): a move's wind-up is its tell; a hit then is a counter hit (x1.4, longer stagger, event 'counter') that cancels it; two contact moves active and in reach both land (trade, x0.7 knockback, event 'trade'); the same move among your last 5 landed hits does 10 % less each (at most half; sub-hits never go stale on their own); hitstun 0.22 s + kb / 1200 (or the move's `stun`), x (1 - 0.1 per combo hit), at least x0.5; knockdown when the move says `kd` or knockback after weight exceeds `RULES.downKb`; down 0.4 s, getting up 0.15 s untouchable, then safe 0.4 s (a hit staggers instead of re-flooring); someone down takes half damage from at most 2 hits a knockdown and can roll up early. A move that met nothing with `contact` or `whiff` sends 'whiff'. Holds (Pyth's coil, Ferris's claw) put the foe in 'grabbed' (`hold` both ways, `F.release`).
