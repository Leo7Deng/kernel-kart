# Kernel Kart: balance

## Balance
Lap time alone is too narrow. Balance means: racers forgive bad driving differently, weight wins and loses shoves in a pack, and items have to actually work (catch-up items catch up, leader items don't run away with the race). When judging a change, look at item value by position, sloppy versus sharp driving, contact, and outcome spread (win %, comebacks), with confidence ranges. Not just average times.

Racers don't need equal numbers. Use `npm run balance -- 640` before judging places: at the default 160 races the ± range is about 0.3. They need a **reason**: every racer in `CHARACTERS` (`js/core.js`) has a `balance` note saying why it's faster or slower where it is, grounded in its stats and traits. `node test/balance.mjs` prints each note next to the numbers and flags a missing one. Nothing is a hard pass/fail. When a racer's numbers contradict its note (slow where the note says fast, or a gap well outside the ± range), fix the racer or rewrite the note. When you change stats, update the note. Two things to know when tuning (2026-10-22): the plain run has no drifting at all (bots drift only in a drift-on race), while drift is on in the game by default, so check `--drift` too (it favours the top-speed cars); and any stat change reshuffles every race, so an unchanged racer's place moves by about ±0.1 from run to run: before moving a racer on one run, see it in another (the same races on other seeds, or drift on and off).

## Front-runner score (per track)
Some maps favour whoever leads more than others (the user's call: that's fine, but show it). `npm run frontrun` (`test/frontrun.mjs`) rates each track 0-10 (10: the leader is very hard to catch) and writes `js/frontrun-data.js`; the menu shows it as a meter with a one-line reason under each track's Show more (`js/frontrun.js`, one hook in ui.js `trackText`).
- **Races** (96 a track by default, ~22 min on 4 workers with the machine busy: seeded all-bot races with items, L0-L2 in turn, 4/6/8 karts, random grids): the **hold rate** (of the karts leading at 1/3, 1/2 and 2/3 of the distance, how many win), **lead changes a minute** (a new leader counts once it has held it 2 s, after the first 10%), the leader's margin and its trend, the **hits on whoever leads** (items against the track's hazards, and what each costs its margin over the next 3 s against how the margin moves anyway) and item pickups a kart a minute.
- **Tools** (what a leader can do on purpose that the bots don't): Octo, Docky and Bugdroid alone (L1, no boxes) replayed with a **mine dropped on their line** at each of ~36 spots round a lap (the best two spots, averaged over the three karts so a spot has to work for all of them: a mine before a jump, a crevasse or the torrent), and a **Turbo fired** at each spot (bot-driven or held flat 2.5 s, whichever is quicker): the best two spots over the median one is what a well-placed Turbo is worth (a secret shortcut, a walkway, a wormhole).
- **Score** = 10 × (0.6 hold + 0.15 calm + 0.25 tools), each 0..1 on fixed anchors (so re-running a few tracks stays comparable): hold (the three hold rates' average) 0.2 → 0, 0.6 → 1; lead changes a minute 3 → 0, 1.2 → 1; tools the larger of the best mine spot (2 s → 0, 5 s → 1) and the Turbo edge (0.5 s → 0, 2.5 s → 1). The hold rate leads because it's the outcome itself; the tools part is there because a human leader drops mines and fires Turbos on purpose and the bots don't. The ± is a bootstrap over the races (the tools part held fixed): about ±1.2 at 96 races, so tracks within ~1.5 of each other aren't really different. The reason line is built from the signals that stand out (`reason`).
- First run (2026-10-21, 96 races a track, 20 tracks): Moonshot 8.1 (provisional), Duke's Volcano 6.6, Pipeline Pass 6.5, Ruby Ruins 6.5, Pyth Plateau 6.0, Cryo Caldera 5.9, Neon Overflow 5.9, Cron Canyon 5.6, Epoch Expressway 4.8 (provisional), Crab Harbor 4.7, Async Atoll 4.7, Sakura Stack 4.5, Assembly Line 4.2, Heap Space 4.0, Merge Bay 3.8, Gateway 8080 3.7, Kernel Pretzel 2.4, Recursion Ridge 2.4, Race Condition 2.1, Pharaoh's Jackpot 1.4. In bot races the leader is hit by items 7-23 times a race, so mid-race leaders win only 24-54% of the time; the item pickup rate (9-12 a kart a minute on Merge Bay, Pharaoh's, Gateway 8080, Recursion Ridge and Pyth Plateau against 5-7 elsewhere) goes with most of the low scores (Pyth Plateau is pulled back up by its phoenix secret).
- Re-run a changed track alone: `npm run frontrun -- 96 3,19` (indices or names; the other entries are kept, removed tracks dropped). `--save=raw.json` / `--load=raw.json` keep the raw results to rescore without racing. `PROVISIONAL` in the script marks tracks being reworked as measured (shown as provisional on the card): empty it once they land and re-run them.
